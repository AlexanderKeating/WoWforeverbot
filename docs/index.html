Forever Sherpa

A free guide for your server, from level 1 to launch night and beyond.

Forever Sherpa posts once or twice a day in your server with the countdown to WoW: Forever. Each post has the day's topic written two ways: 🌱 for new players and 🧙 for veterans. You also get a pro tip, the latest news headlines, top clips and a class roll call.

  • Class help: /classtips, /keybinds, /macros, /rotation and a /whichclass quiz.
  • Plus One mode: /draggedalong and /whatdoesthatmean, for anyone whose partner talked them into playing.
  • Your level of detail: Brief, Standard, Detailed or Plain words, with /mysettings.
  • Quiet by default: no @pings, and everything personal is answered privately.
